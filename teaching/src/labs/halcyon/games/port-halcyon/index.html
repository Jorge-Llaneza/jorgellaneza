Your notebook

Numbers worth knowing

  • Contrast: 4.5 : 1 for normal text, 3 : 1 for large text (24 px, or 18.7 px bold).
  • Text: at least 11 px, proportional line spacing, plain fonts, no phrases in capitals.
  • Colour deficiency: 8% of men, 1% of women. Avoid red-green, blue-yellow, green-blue and red-blue pairs.
  • Redundant codes: colour plus shape, icon or text.
  • Fitts’ law: T = 0.1 · log₂(D / A + 1) s. Bigger and closer is faster.
  • Hick’s law: T = 0.15 · log₂(n + 1) s. Every option costs time.
  • Short-term memory: 7 ± 2 items. Never make users retype or recall.
  • Proportional effort: hard to undo means hard to do.
