Hazard log

    Field guide

    RAW: read after write

    A later instruction reads a register an earlier one has not written back yet. This is the hazard the line simulates. Forwarding sends the result from a pipeline latch straight to EX.

    Load-use

    A lw only has its value after MEM. A dependent instruction right behind it needs one stall even with forwarding.

    WAR and WAW

    They cannot happen here: this in-order line always writes registers in WB, in program order. They appear with out-of-order execution and are solved by register renaming.

    Why IPC matters

    TCPU = instructions × CPI × T. Stalls raise CPI, and IPC = 1 / CPI. An ideal pipeline reaches CPI 1.
